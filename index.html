Quiet by design.

One rule

The desktop stays out of the way until it has something to say. Everything that happens on the machine passes through a single island at the top of the screen. It grows when there is news and shrinks back into a pill when there isn't. Closed panels are not loaded at all, and the island goes still while a game runs fullscreen.

Install

Needs pacman and a normal user. The installer detects Arch or Artix and your GPU, copies your current configs to ~/.config/dotfiles-backup-<timestamp>, then builds the colour scheme from your first wallpaper. Add --dry-run to see every step first.

bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fedyaqq34356/rosa/main/install.sh)

Updates arrive as a chip next to the workspaces, or run rosa-update. To leave, run the uninstaller: your old configs come back, packages stay.

bash ~/.local/share/rosa/uninstall.sh
